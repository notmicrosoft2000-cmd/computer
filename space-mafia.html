Space Mafia

An Among Us-style murder mystery that runs in your DMs.

The lobby is in a channel. The game happens in your DMs.

Run /sus new in any channel and players press join. When the host presses begin, everyone gets a role card and a live control panel in their DMs: their room, who is standing nearby, their task list, cooldowns and sabotage timers.

Crewmates finish tasks or vote out every impostor. Impostors win by killing down to parity, or by letting the reactor blow. Bots can fill the empty seats and argue right back.

Game phase

  • Lobby
  • Discussion
  • Voting
  • Results
    •

    Players join, the host pads the crew with bots (+ bot / − bot), pings the server with ping players, then hits begin.

    A body is reported or the emergency button is hit. Everyone gathers in the cafeteria; chat lines relay into every living player's DMs while the discussion timer runs.

    Vote menus slide into living players' DMs. Majority ejects; ties and skip-majority eject nobody. Then it is back to tasks.

    A victory card posts in the channel with the full case file: role reveal, kill timeline, kill-cam reel and voting record. One button starts a rematch.

    Demo note: phase tabs and seat counts above are local to your browser.

    Crewmates, impostors, and the ones in between

    Crewmate Does tasks tied to rooms, reports bodies, and gets one emergency button.

    Impostor Kills, vents, sabotages and fakes tasks. The second impostor is always a Shapeshifter.

    Special roles unlock as the ship fills: Scientist at 4 players, Engineer at 5, Guardian Angel at 6, Sheriff at 7, Marionette at 8, Jester at 9. Bots play the special roles too.

    Rooms, sabotage and minigames

    The ship

    • 14 rooms connected by corridors; each hop takes about 6 seconds by default.
    • Vents are instant teleports, for Engineers and impostors only.
    • Sabotages (shared 60s cooldown): lights out, comms down, sealed doors, reactor meltdown.
    • Bodies stay where they fall until somebody walks in and reports them.
    • Cameras in Security and the Admin table give live, refreshing views.

    Tasks are real minigames

    • Fix Wiring, Swipe Card, Unlock Manifolds, Calibrate Distributor, Align Engine Output.
    • Clear Asteroids, Stabilize Steering, Chart Course, Download / Upload, Inspect Sample.
    • Clean O2 Filter, Water Plants, Sort Records, Charge Laptop, Reset Breakers, Fuel Engines.
    • Short tasks finish where you solve them; long tasks make you stay put.
    • Impostors get the same minigames with zero progress on the bar.

    The task bar counts completed steps, not whole tasks.

    Tune the voyage

    The host opens /sus settings for an interactive panel. Settings are saved per channel and apply to every new game, with some applied live.

    SettingRange / valuesMeaning
    kill_cooldown10–60 sSeconds between impostor kills.
    walk_seconds3–15 sTime per room-to-room hop.
    tasks_per_crew2–8Tasks each crewmate must finish.
    long_task_seconds6–30 sHow long a long task holds you in place.
    ai_chatteron / offWhether bots use AI-generated chat (needs a key).
    impostors1–3Force the impostor count.
    sabotage_cooldown15–120 sSeconds between sabotages.

    A preset button cycles Quick → Classic → Slow burn in one tap.

    Running a game

    CommandWhoWhat it does
    /sus newanyoneOpen a lobby in this channel.
    /sus bots nhostSet the bot-player count directly (lobby only).
    /sus settingshostOpen the house-rules panel.
    /sus panelplayersRe-send your control panel to your DMs.
    /sus taskplayersList your remaining (or fake) tasks.
    /sus spectateanyoneGet a sanitized spectator feed in your DMs.
    /sus endhostAbort the running game.

    From lobby to case file

    1. Lobby — /sus new, players join, the host adds bots and begins.
    2. Play — walk the ship, do tasks, watch the bar fill. Impostors stalk and sabotage.
    3. Report — a body or the emergency button gathers everyone and starts discussion.
    4. Vote — menus open in DMs; majority decides who gets ejected.
    5. Win — crew by tasks or ejections; impostors by parity or a blown reactor.
    6. Rematch — the victory card rebuilds the lobby with who stayed.
