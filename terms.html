4. YOUR SERVER'S RESPONSIBILITY

This is the section admins need to read, so it's the blunt one.

SERVERS ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR THEIR USERS

The Bot relays messages between real servers run by strangers. Individual users can't be blocked by us reliably — they're anonymous behind a server. That means your server's staff are the enforcement point.

  • You are responsible for the content relayed through your phone channel and for the behaviour of anyone using your line.
  • Register a channel you're willing to open up. Everything sent to your number appears in the channel you registered. Don't register a staff-only or moderation channel.
  • Staff must respond to complaints. If your users repeatedly break the code of conduct and staff don't act, the entire server can be blacklisted from the network.
  • Unregistering isn't a shield. Removing the Bot, or unregistering your line, ends phone access but doesn't erase the record. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Keep the channel clean. Set the filters, use block lists, and check who you grant trusts to.

Content filters are ON by default for profanity, slurs, links, and GIFs. Any server can trust another to skip filtering. Trusting someone you don't know means their messages bypass your filters entirely.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT — ALL USERS

This is the text every server accepts when it joins the phone network:

  1. No slurs, hate speech, or harassment — ever. Zero tolerance.
  2. No spam, flooding, or link-dumping — through the wire or in voicemails.
  3. No inappropriate content for minors — this is a public line.
  4. No impersonation, doxxing, or sharing personal information.
  5. Users are responsible for their own messages — but the server is responsible for its users. If a user breaks these rules repeatedly and staff don't act, the whole server can be blacklisted.
  6. Reports matter. /phone report after a bad call saves the transcript and flags it to operators. Don't abuse it.
  7. Admins may blacklist servers that break the rules or shelter users who do.

Breaking rule 1, 3, or 4 leads to immediate disconnection from the network. Breaking the others gets warnings first, unless it's serious.

6. HOW WE MODERATE

Enforcement is a judgement call by a very small team of operators. Here's how it actually works, so there are no surprises:

  • Reports first. /phone report captures the transcript of the call in progress. That transcript is what operators read. Without a report, we generally have nothing to look at.
  • Evidence beats vibes. Operators act on transcripts, not on someone complaining that a server is "toxic."
  • Graduated response. Warnings, filter tightening, then blacklisting. Immediate blacklisting for slurs, hate speech, or sexual content involving minors.
  • False reports are a violation. Filing a report to harass a server you dislike gets your server cut too.
  • Appeals. A blacklisted server can appeal through the official Discord server. Appeals are reviewed by an operator who wasn't involved in the original call, where possible.

Operators are volunteers reading this in their spare time. If we take a few days to answer an appeal, that's the honest reason, not a policy.

7. REPORTING AND TAKEDOWN

If something happens that shouldn't — harassment, hate speech, or content involving minors — here's the fastest path:

  1. Report it to Discord via discord.com/report. For anything involving minors, go straight to Discord and to law enforcement. Do not wait for us.
  2. Report the call in Discord with /phone report while it's happening — that preserves the transcript.
  3. Contact us using the details in Section 14, including your server name and, if you have it, the other server's number.
  4. Tell your own server's staff so they can act on their end.

WHAT A REPORT CAPTURES

A call report stores the full transcript of the call, meaning the messages of every participant, not just the person you're reporting. That's deliberate — a transcript nobody keeps can't be reviewed — but you should know it before you file one.

We'll act on clear abuse, and we don't require you to have been personally insulted first. Threats, doxxing, and content involving minors get acted on the same day, weekends included.

9. AI-GENERATED CONTENT

Bot players can be powered by a language model. When that happens:

  • Bot lines are machine-generated and may be inaccurate, repetitive, offensive, or nonsensical. Don't treat them as statements by a real person.
  • Bots can hallucinate. They are a party game mechanic, not a source of truth. Never rely on a bot's claim about who's innocent.
  • Meeting chat is sent to a third-party AI provider for this purpose. Full detail, and how to opt out, is in Privacy Policy Section 9.
  • You consent to this by enabling it — either as a server admin, or by playing on a server where it's enabled. If you don't want your chat sent to a third party, opt out via /sus privacy, or ask your admin to disable it server-wide.

AI chat is off unless configured. With it off, bots use fixed scripted lines and no third party is involved at all.

10. YOUR DATA

Full detail is in the Privacy Policy. The short version of your rights here:

  • /sus export — see everything held about you, as a summary plus raw JSON.
  • /sus forget — erase your profile, release your extension, reset your stats. Irreversible.
  • /sus privacy — view and change your AI-sharing setting.
  • /phone forget — staff can unregister a listing and purge that server's call history and voicemails.

Nothing requires emailing anyone or filing a form. If you'd rather do it by hand, that's fine too — see Section 14.
