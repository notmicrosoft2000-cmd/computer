THE SHORT VERSION

Computer stores very little, because it has to. There are no accounts with passwords, no email addresses, no payment info, no analytics, and no advertising.

  • Game: your Discord user ID, your display name, and a handful of preferences and career counters.
  • Telephone: per-server settings and a call history. Never message bodies, except voicemails and call reports.
  • AI bots: if AI bot chat is switched on, some meeting chat lines are sent to Groq. You can opt out — see Section 9.
  • You can export or erase your data from inside Discord, any time. See Section 11.

1. WHAT THIS COVERS

Computer is one Discord bot doing three separate jobs. This policy covers all three, because they share a database:

  • The murder-mystery game — /sus. The lobby lives in a server channel; the actual game is played through Discord DMs.
  • The cross-server telephone — /phone. Connects real Discord servers to each other over one shared phone network.
  • The ship phone — /user. Lets players ring each other by 4-digit extension through DMs.

Discord itself is a separate company with its own privacy policy. Anything Discord holds — including your DMs with the bot — is subject to Discord's policy, not this one. We only control what our own database files hold.

2. WHO RUNS IT

Computer is an independent project published by Neptune Productions under the Discord user neptunetheii (GitHub: notmicrosoft2000-cmd). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

The bot's source code is currently kept private, so this page is the authoritative description of what is stored. It is a Python discord.py bot; if the code is ever published, this policy will link to it.

THE ONE THING WORTH UNDERSTANDING

The telephone relays messages between unrelated servers run by strangers. If your server registers a phone jack, everyone on the network can ring you and send text into that channel. That is the entire point of the feature — but it means you are choosing to expose that channel to strangers.

Use a dedicated channel you don't mind people posting in. Do not register a channel you also use for private staff talk.

5. WHAT IS ONLY IN MEMORY

Not written to disk, and lost whenever the bot restarts:

  • Active games: player names, roles, room positions, chat logs, kill logs, voting records.
  • Live call transcripts — the messages exchanged during a call currently in progress. These are held in memory and dropped an hour after the call ends, unless somebody filed a report.
  • Who is ringing whom, and who is in the party bridge.

This is good news for privacy. It's also why a crash loses in-flight games. We consider that an acceptable trade.

6. WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

  • Directly from you — preferences, pronouns, catchphrase, custom name, accent colour, your I AGREE click on the code of conduct, opt-outs, and any /phone report you file.
  • From Discord — your user ID and display name, and your server's ID, name, and channel IDs. The bot cannot function without these.
  • From your server's staff — a member of staff runs /phone register and can set filters, DND, auto-answer, block servers, and pick alert roles. They are not required to and are not asked to collect anything else.
  • From other servers — when your server is relayed to, or receives a call from, another server. Their messages arrive in your channel under their own names. We don't add anything to those messages.

Scope of what we can read: in servers, the bot reads messages only in a registered telephone jack channel. It does not read other channels. It does not read your DMs except the DMs it is itself sending you. It does not keep a log of anything it can see.

7. WHAT WE USE IT FOR

Strictly to operate the features you asked for:

  • Running games and keeping score.
  • Giving you a phone number so other players can ring you.
  • Routing calls, holding voicemails, and applying your content filters.
  • Showing you your own stats when you ask with /sus account.
  • Stopping abuse: enforcing the code of conduct, blacklisting servers, and reading transcripts a server has explicitly reported.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or broker your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not build profiles. We do not run automated decision-making that affects you.

8. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We share data with three kinds of party, and no others:

  1. Discord — the whole game runs on Discord's infrastructure: DMs, channels, webhooks, reactions. Discord processes all of it under their own policy.
  2. Groq — only if AI bot chat is enabled, and only as described in Section 9.
  3. Our hosting provider — the bot runs on a small host that stores these files and executes the code. They host it; they don't inspect it.

THIS IS THE ONE THAT SURPRISES PEOPLE

Relayed phone messages are posted into the receiving server through a webhook wearing the sender's name and avatar. That is how a message from #1234-A can appear to come from someone in #1234-B. Your server admins can see this. It is a design choice, not a bug — but you should know the receiving side cannot tell a relayed message apart from a locally-typed one by appearance alone.

We do not respond to third-party requests for your data, and we cannot hand over anything Discord holds — we don't have it either.

9. AI AND GROQ — READ THIS ONE

Computer's bots can be powered by a language model instead of a fixed script. When that is on, your meeting chat is sent to Groq, a third-party AI provider, so their model can write a bot's reply. This is the most significant data flow on this page, so it gets its own section.

Exactly what is sent

  • Up to the last 14 lines of meeting chat, each with the display name of who said it. Bot lines are included. Human lines are included.
  • Up to 200 characters of the message that triggered the bot's reply, quoted with the sender's display name.
  • The bot's own role, personality, behaviour state, and target.
  • Names of players a bot has decided it dislikes.

What is never sent

  • Anything from outside the game channel. Task answers, panel clicks, phone calls, voicemails, call logs, and reports are never sent to the AI provider.
  • Your Discord user ID, email, or any account data. Only display names appear in prompts.
  • Anything at all, if AI is not configured — in which case bots use built-in scripted lines and function normally.

Your controls

  • Personal opt-out — /sus privacy ai:true. Your chat lines are removed entirely from AI prompts, and when you're the one who set a bot off, it's told only that a player spoke and that the content was withheld — no name, no quote. The bot still talks; it just can't quote you.
  • Server-wide opt-out — staff run /sus privacy server_ai:true, and nothing from that server reaches a third party. This setting persists across restarts, so a deploy can't quietly undo it.
  • Global opt-out — the operator can disable AI for the entire network with SUS_AI_ENABLED=false, which stops all AI calls code-path-wide.
  • Three independent layers. Your opt-out is checked on every prompt, not just when you set it, so it applies mid-game and doesn't depend on a flag someone forgot to flip.

Groq's own handling of API traffic is governed by Groq's privacy policy. Please read it if the details matter to you.

11. YOUR RIGHTS

You can exercise these yourself, from inside Discord. You do not need to email anyone or file a form.

  • See everything we hold about you — /sus export sends you a readable summary plus the raw JSON of your profile.
  • Erase it — /sus forget deletes your profile, releases your ship extension, removes your ring-alert subscription, and resets your stats. This cannot be undone.
  • Stop AI sharing — /sus privacy shows your current settings and lets you opt out.
  • Check the AI status of your server — /sus privacy reports whether the host has AI chat on.
  • Server-side export — staff can run /phone data export to DM themselves a JSON dump of everything the network holds about their server.
  • Server-side erasure — staff can run /phone data forget to wipe their listing, number, filters, trusts, blocks, speed dials, ring alerts, call history, voicemails, and any reports filed by or about the server. Blacklist entries are kept — that's the operator's record of a decision, not your data.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian can ask us to erase a profile by making the same request. Just say so in the contact below and we'll take the word for it rather than interrogating a minor about their data rights.

Stats are cumulative counters with no backup of past games, so forgetting you resets the record with nothing lost in the void. The ship extension does become available for reassignment.

12. MODERATION AND SAFETY

The telephone connects strangers. That creates obvious risks, so here is the whole enforcement model:

  • Every server accepts a code of conduct once, by clicking I AGREE, before any phone command works.
  • Content filters for profanity, slurs, links, and GIFs run by default. A server can trust another server to skip filtering, but that trust is mutual and revocable.
  • /phone report saves the transcript of the call in progress and flags the involved servers for operators. Operators read these with /phone data reports — the reports were always reviewable, not just collected.
  • Operators can blacklist a server, which blocks its phone outright, tears down any call in progress, and stops it relaying messages.

Two things to be honest about:

  • A report stores the full transcript of the call, including messages from every participant. Filing one captures other people's messages too. That is the point — a transcript nobody keeps can't be reviewed — but it does mean a report is not a private note to staff.
  • Enforcement is ultimately on server staff. If your server's members break the rules and staff don't act, the whole server can be cut off. Choose servers you trust, or run one with moderation you actually pay attention to.

13. CHILDREN

Computer is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. Discord's minimum age is 13 in most countries. The game is playable by teens and adults; the telephone network is explicitly a public space where the code of conduct forbids content inappropriate for minors, and violations get a server blacklisted.

If you believe a child under 13 has used the bot, contact us and we'll erase the profile.

Because the bot's source is public, you can always read the code and see for yourself what is stored. If this page and the code ever disagree, the code is the truth and this page is the bug.
